Home volumes

The disks mounted at $HOME in your VM sessions. Each instance gets its own by default; naming one lets a home follow you between instances. A volume is attached to one running instance at a time — a home carries an ext4 filesystem, which cannot be attached to two running guests at once — so stop one instance before starting another that uses the same home.
