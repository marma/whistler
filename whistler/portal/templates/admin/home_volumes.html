Home volumes (all users)

Every named home across the cluster. Each is a disk image on its own claim, mounted at $HOME in a VM session. A volume is attached to one running instance at a time — a home carries an ext4 filesystem, which cannot be attached to two running guests at once. Zones comes straight from each user's access matrix: it is where the volume may actually be mounted, and nowhere else records it.

Archived

Homes taken away from their users, disk and data intact. An archived volume belongs to nobody and cannot be mounted. Restore gives it to a user as a new home volume (grant it a zone on their page afterwards: the access matrix has no defaults); Delete destroys the data.
