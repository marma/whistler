Volumes & Datasets

What members may mount, and how. What read-only is worth depends on which kind it is: for a dataset it is enforced by the proxy, so it holds even in a VM where the user is root. Access

Which datasets this group's members may mount, and in which zone. A member's effective access is this unioned with their own, most permissive per cell — joining a project confers access, it never takes any away. Home volumes are not here on purpose: a home's name only resolves in its owner's own namespace, so a group could never grant one to anybody. Groups grant shared data.
