Access

Which datasets this group's members may mount, and in which zone — the one table that decides it, now that the separate allow-list with its per-member exceptions is gone. A member's effective access is this unioned with their own, most permissive per cell: joining a project confers access, it never takes any away. To hand one person something the rest of the project does not get, grant it on their User rather than here. Home volumes are not here on purpose: a home's name only resolves in its owner's own namespace, so a group could never grant one to anybody. Groups grant shared data.

Read-only on a dataset is enforced by the proxy Whistler runs in front of it, so it holds even in a VM where the member is root (design/security.md, design/storage.md).
